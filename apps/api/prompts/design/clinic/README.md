Design reference for clinics and healthcare practices, distilled from two professional clinic
templates. The screenshots in this folder show the look to match: `reference-hero.jpg` is the
hero, `reference-full-page.jpg` is an overview of a full home page.

## Palette

- Background white `#ffffff`; alternate sections on a soft blue tint `#f4f8ff` (or `#eff5ff`).
- Accent: confident medical blue `#175cdd` (hover `#0f4fc4`) for buttons, links, icons, stats.
- Headings: deep navy `#112344`. Body text: slate `#3c4049`. Muted text: `#6b7280`.
- Footer: dark navy `#1b2c51` with white and 70%-white text.
- Use a top utility bar in the accent blue with email, phone and social icons in white.

## Typography

- Headings: **Montserrat**, weight 700–800, tight tracking, large hero (56–64px desktop).
- Body: **Roboto** 400/500, 16–17px, relaxed line height.
- Highlight one key word in the hero heading in the accent colour with an underline
  (e.g. "Excellence in <u>Healthcare</u> With Compassionate Care").

## Layout & components (in this order)

1. **Top bar** (accent blue, small text) → **sticky white header** with the clinic name as a
   bold wordmark, nav links (active link underlined in blue) and an "Appointment" button.
2. **Hero, two columns.** Left: row of pill trust badges (light blue background, blue text and
   icon — "Accredited", "24/7 Emergency", "4.9/5 Rating"), big heading, short paragraph, a stats
   row (big blue numbers like "15+ Years Experience", "5000+ Patients Treated", "50+ Medical
   Experts"), a solid pill "Book Appointment" button plus an outlined "Watch Our Story" button,
   and an "Emergency Hotline" card with a round blue phone icon. Right: a large rounded photo of
   a smiling doctor, decorated with soft blurred blue circles, a floating white card
   "Next Available — Today 2:30 PM — Dr. Sarah Johnson" with a calendar icon (top-right), and a
   floating rating card "★★★★★ 4.9/5 · 1,234 Reviews" (bottom-left).
3. **About / Why trust us**: overlapping photo pair on one side; on the other a small eyebrow
   label, heading, paragraph, check-list ("Quality health care", "Only qualified doctors",
   "Medical research professionals") and a "Read More" button.
4. **Services / Departments grid**: 3×2 cards on the tinted background — white cards, rounded
   corners, light blue icon square, title (Cardiology, Pulmonary, Neurology, Orthopedics, Dental
   Surgery, Laboratory), two-line description, small arrow link.
5. **Why choose us** band: full-width accent-blue section with photo on one side and a 2×2 grid of
   white-icon features ("Experienced Doctors", "Quality Services", "Positive Consultation",
   "24 Hours Support").
6. **Doctors**: four portrait cards with photo, name, specialty and small social icons.
7. **Appointment**: two columns — contact details (phone, email) on the left, a form card on the
   right (name, email, phone, doctor select, date, time, message, full-width "Book Appointment").
8. **Testimonials**: centred quote card with round avatar, patient name and profession.
9. **Footer** (dark navy): Address, Services, Quick Links and Newsletter columns.

## Details

- Corner radius: 12–16px for cards and images, fully rounded (pill) buttons and badges.
- Shadows: soft and wide (`0 20px 50px -20px rgba(17,35,68,.25)`), never harsh.
- Icons: simple line icons in the accent blue inside light blue rounded squares.
- Motion: gentle fade-up on scroll; cards lift slightly on hover.
- Tone of copy: calm, reassuring, professional; emphasise same-day appointments, insurance,
  experienced doctors and a caring team.
